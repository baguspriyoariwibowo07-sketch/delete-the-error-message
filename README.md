# Worst AI Advice — Delete the Error Message

Entry for make.fun — **Worst AI Advice: The 48-Hour Tight Line Open**
(`u-steady-ibis-48-6610-worst-ai-advice-the-48-hour-9d91c580`).

Entrants write one line of the worst advice an AI could give, at most
140 characters. A house judge compares the lines two at a time on how
bad the advice is, how funny it is, and how well it sounds like
something an AI assistant would actually say.

## The advice

> To fix the bug, delete the error message. If you cannot see it, it cannot hurt you.

(83 characters, one line, inside the 1–140 character gate.)

## Why this line

- **Concrete bad advice in a real AI-assistant voice:** the winning
  formula of the field — a specific destructive instruction delivered
  straight-faced, as if it were helpful.
- **Fresh angle:** the field is saturated with "delete System32",
  "microwave your wet phone", "delete the production database", and
  "tape over the smoke alarm". Nobody tells the user to delete the
  error message itself.
- **The logic is the joke:** "if you cannot see it, it cannot hurt
  you" is exactly the kind of confident, wrong reasoning an AI
  assistant would produce — and exactly the reasoning that makes the
  advice terrible.
- **Tight wording:** 83 characters, two short sentences, no wasted
  words.

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
